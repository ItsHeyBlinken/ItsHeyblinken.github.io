FAQ

Common questions from business owners.

Straight answers about the Website Starter, the monthly fee, and what happens after launch.

How long until my site is live?

Most Website Starter sites are ready in about one to two weeks once we have your business name, services, photos, and contact details. You review it before it goes live.

What does the $50/month cover?

Hosting, SSL security, basic maintenance, technical support, and small text or image updates. You do not have to log into a website builder or keep the site online yourself.

Is this a do-it-yourself website?

No. We build a professional site for your business and keep it running. You send us the updates you need—hours, phone number, a new photo—and we handle them.

What if I need more than small updates?

New pages, new features, a redesign, online ordering, or a bigger project are quoted separately. The monthly plan is for hosting, care, and everyday content changes—not unlimited extra development.

How do I get started?

Email us. Tell us your business name, what you do, and your service area. We reply within 48 hours with next steps. Prefer a call? Say so in the email.

Does the monthly plan include SEO?

It includes basic on-page setup when we build the site. It does not include ongoing SEO campaigns. If you want help getting found on Google later, we can talk about that as an add-on—without promising rankings.
